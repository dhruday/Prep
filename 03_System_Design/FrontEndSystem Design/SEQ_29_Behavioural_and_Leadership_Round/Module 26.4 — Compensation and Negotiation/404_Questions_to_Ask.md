# 404 – Questions to Ask the Interviewer — Strategic Impressions

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

## 1. ⚡ HIGH-LEVEL EXPLANATION
The questions YOU ask reveal more than answers you give. Ask about **team impact**, **technical challenges**, **growth**, and **culture**. Avoid salary/perks in early rounds. Show you're evaluating them as much as they're evaluating you.

## 2. 🔬 DEEP-DIVE EXPLANATION

### Tier 1: Technical/Role Questions (ask engineers)
```
"What's the most interesting technical challenge your team is 
 currently solving?"

"What does your frontend tech stack look like? Any migrations 
 or modernization planned?"

"How do you handle frontend architecture decisions — ADRs, 
 RFCs, or team discussions?"

"What does your testing strategy look like — unit, integration, 
 E2E coverage targets?"

"How do you balance tech debt vs. new features?"
```

### Tier 2: Team/Culture Questions (ask managers)
```
"How is the team structured? How many frontend engineers?"

"What does a successful first 90 days look like in this role?"

"How do you support engineers' growth — conferences, 
 learning budget, internal mobility?"

"What's the biggest challenge the team faced recently, 
 and how did you handle it?"

"How do feedback cycles work — how often do engineers 
 get performance feedback?"
```

### Tier 3: Strategic Questions (ask leadership/hiring manager)
```
"Where do you see this product/team in 2 years?"

"How does this team's work connect to the company's 
 broader strategy?"

"What's the team's deployment cadence and what would 
 you like it to be?"
```

### Questions to AVOID
```
❌ "What does the company do?" (shows no research)
❌ "What's the salary?" (too early — wait for offer stage)
❌ "How many vacation days?" (signals wrong priorities)
❌ "Can I work from home full time?" (ask later, not round 1)
❌ "Did I get the job?" (awkward and pressuring)
```

### Context-Sensitive Questions
| If Interview Was... | Ask... |
|---|---|
| System design round | "How do you evaluate architecture tradeoffs in practice?" |
| Coding round | "What's the code review culture like?" |
| Behavioral round | "How does the team handle conflict or disagreements?" |
| With a skip-level | "What distinguishes top performers on your team?" |

## 3. 🎯 KEY TAKEAWAY
**"Ask 2-3 thoughtful questions per round. Technical questions to engineers, culture questions to managers. Show curiosity about challenges and growth. The questions you ask are an interview within the interview."**
