# 403 – Salary Negotiation Tactics — Anchoring, Counter-Offers, Timing

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

## 1. ⚡ HIGH-LEVEL EXPLANATION
Never accept the first offer. Use **anchoring** (name a high number first), **silence** (let them fill the gap), and **compete offers** (multiple offers = leverage). Negotiate AFTER the offer, not before. The recruiter's job is to close you — they expect negotiation.

## 2. 🔬 DEEP-DIVE EXPLANATION

### Golden Rules
```
1. NEVER share your current salary (it's irrelevant to market value)
2. NEVER name a number first if you can avoid it
3. Always negotiate — they expect it and budget for it
4. Get everything in writing before accepting
5. Be enthusiastic but firm: "I'm excited, but the number needs to work"
```

### Handling "What's Your Expected Salary?"
```
EARLY IN PROCESS (deflect):
"I'm more focused on finding the right role and team fit.
I trust [Company] compensates competitively. Can we revisit
compensation once we've established mutual fit?"

IF PRESSED:
"Based on my research and experience, I'm targeting a total
compensation in the range of [X-Y LPA]. But I'm flexible for
the right opportunity."
→ Name a range where your FLOOR is your actual target
```

### After Receiving an Offer
```
Step 1: THANK THEM enthusiastically
  "Thank you! I'm very excited about this opportunity."

Step 2: ASK FOR TIME
  "I'd love to review the full offer details. Can I have 
  3-4 days to consider?"

Step 3: EVALUATE the full package
  Base + Bonus + RSU vesting schedule + signing + benefits

Step 4: COUNTER with justification
  "I'm very excited about joining. Based on my research on 
  Levels.fyi and similar offers I've received, I was hoping 
  for [X] in base and [Y] in RSU. Given my [specific 
  experience], I believe this reflects fair market value."

Step 5: NEGOTIATE non-salary if salary is firm
  → Signing bonus, RSU refresh, remote flexibility, 
     start date, learning budget, title
```

### Negotiation Levers
| Lever | When to Use |
|---|---|
| Base salary | Always try first |
| RSU/Stock | When base is capped by band |
| Signing bonus | One-time, easier for companies to approve |
| Early review | "6-month performance review with salary adjustment" |
| Title/Level | If offer is at lower level than expected |
| Start date | Need time for notice period or personal prep |
| Remote/hybrid | Valuable and increasingly negotiable |

### The Power of Competing Offers
```
"I really want to join [Company]. I have another offer at [X LPA]
from [Other Company], but [Company] is my first choice. Is there
flexibility to match/exceed that?"

→ Never lie about competing offers — they may verify
→ Multiple offers = strongest negotiating position
→ Apply broadly, interview in parallel, collect offers simultaneously
```

## 3. 🎯 KEY TAKEAWAY
**"Never accept first offer. Anchor high. Justify with data (Levels.fyi + competing offers). Negotiate base first, then stock, then signing bonus. Always be enthusiastic — they want to close you."**
