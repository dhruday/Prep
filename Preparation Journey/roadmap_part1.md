## Roadmap Philosophy

### Why This Sequence Works

As a Hiring Committee member at Big Tech, I've seen hundreds of engineers fail not because they lacked knowledge — but because they prepared topics in the wrong order, left system design too late, or crammed behavioral prep in the final week.

This roadmap is built on three principles:

**1. Interview Readiness From Day 1**
By Day 10, you will be able to walk into a frontend interview and perform competently. Every subsequent day makes you sharper. There is no "cliff" where readiness suddenly jumps.

**2. Spiral Learning**
Topics aren't studied once and abandoned. Core concepts (JavaScript internals, rendering performance, system design) resurface multiple times across 90 days — each time at greater depth. Spaced repetition is baked in.

**3. Senior-Level Framing**
At L5/L6, interviewers don't want you to list features — they want to see *judgment*. Every day's agenda is framed around the question: "How would a Staff Engineer explain this trade-off?"
