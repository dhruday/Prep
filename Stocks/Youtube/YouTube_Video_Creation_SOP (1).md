## 1. Video Idea Validation

> **Goal:** Only create videos that people are already searching for or emotionally invested in.

### Demand Check
- [ ] Search the topic on YouTube — are there already videos with 50K+ views on this topic?
- [ ] Check Google Trends India for the keyword — is it rising or stable?
- [ ] Search the topic on YouTube in Telugu — how many results appear? (Fewer = opportunity)
- [ ] Would a 30-year-old Telugu investor feel anxiety, excitement, or curiosity about this topic?
- [ ] Can you explain the core idea in one sentence? (If not, the topic is too vague)

### Audience Fit Check
- [ ] Does this topic serve your bullseye audience — Telugu investors aged 25–40 doing SIPs who want to find wealth-building stocks?
- [ ] Is this topic about a company or concept most people already know? (Known = higher CTR)
- [ ] Does this topic have a clear outcome the viewer wants — money made, money saved, mistake avoided?

### Content Type Check
- [ ] Is this a **search-based** topic? (People are typing it into YouTube)
- [ ] Is this a **browse-based** topic? (People will click it in their feed because it's emotional)
- [ ] Is this an **evergreen** topic? (Will it be relevant 2 years from now?)

> **Tip:** Prioritize search-based and evergreen topics until you reach 10,000 subscribers. Browse-based content works better once you have an established audience.

### ✅ Idea Passes If:
- [ ] At least 2 of the 3 content type boxes apply
- [ ] You can state the emotional hook in under 10 words
- [ ] There is search demand in Telugu or the topic is trending in Indian finance news

## 2. Research Process

> **Goal:** Know more about this topic than your viewer before you hit record.

### Source Research
- [ ] Read the company's latest annual report or quarterly result (for stock analysis)
- [ ] Check NSE/BSE filings page for official data
- [ ] Read 3 analyst reports or financial news articles (ET, Moneycontrol, Livemint)
- [ ] Note all key numbers: revenue, profit, PE ratio, debt, promoter holding
- [ ] Find one surprising or counterintuitive fact — this becomes your hook

### Competitor Research
- [ ] Search the exact title you plan to use on YouTube
- [ ] Watch the top 3 competing videos — what are they missing?
- [ ] Note what angle they took — choose a different or more specific angle
- [ ] Check the comments on competing videos — what questions are viewers asking?

### Data Collection
- [ ] Screenshot or save all charts, tables, and graphs you will reference
- [ ] Note the source and date for every data point (for description credibility)
- [ ] Find 1–2 comparison examples that make the numbers feel real to a common investor

> **Example:** Instead of "Revenue grew 22%", say "If you had invested ₹1 lakh in 2020, it would be ₹2.4 lakh today."

## 3. Script Writing Checklist

> **Goal:** A script that keeps viewers watching from start to finish.

### Structure
- [ ] Hook written first (see Section 4)
- [ ] Stakes established in first 60 seconds — why should the viewer care?
- [ ] Story or narrative arc — not just data points
- [ ] Maximum 3 main points per video (more = viewer confusion)
- [ ] Each section ends with a micro-curiosity that pulls to the next section
- [ ] Clear verdict or recommendation at the end
- [ ] Call to action in the final 30 seconds

### Script Quality
- [ ] Script is written in conversational Telugu — as if talking to a friend
- [ ] No sentence is longer than 15 words
- [ ] Every technical term is immediately explained with a simple analogy
- [ ] At least 1 real-life example per main point
- [ ] Numbers are always given with context ("₹500 crore — that's the same as buying 10,000 average Indian homes")

### Length Guidelines
| Video Type | Ideal Duration | Script Word Count |
|---|---|---|
| Stock analysis | 12–18 minutes | 1,800–2,700 words |
| Mutual fund review | 10–14 minutes | 1,500–2,100 words |
| Personal finance topic | 8–12 minutes | 1,200–1,800 words |
| Weekly market recap | 6–10 minutes | 900–1,500 words |
| Beginner masterclass | 20–35 minutes | 3,000–5,000 words |

> **Tip:** Speak at 130–150 words per minute on camera. Use this to estimate your recording time from the word count.

## 4. Hook Creation

> **Goal:** The first 30 seconds must make the viewer think "I need to watch all of this."

### Hook Formula
The best hooks follow one of these patterns:

**Pattern 1 — The Shocking Statement**
> "90% of SIP investors are making a mistake right now — and their bank is hoping they never find out."

**Pattern 2 — The Specific Number**
> "If you had invested ₹10,000 in this stock in 2020, today you would have ₹87,000. And barely anyone knows this company."

**Pattern 3 — The Common Mistake**
> "You are probably comparing mutual funds the wrong way. Here is what actually matters."

**Pattern 4 — The Urgent Question**
> "Nifty crossed 25,000 last week. What does that mean for your SIP? The answer might surprise you."

**Pattern 5 — The Counterintuitive Truth**
> "The stock everyone is buying right now is the one you should probably avoid."

### Hook Checklist
- [ ] Hook is written and locked before anything else in the script
- [ ] Hook states the most surprising or valuable thing in the entire video
- [ ] Hook does NOT start with "Hello everyone, welcome to Telugu Smart Investor" — get to the point immediately
- [ ] Hook is 20–40 seconds long maximum
- [ ] Hook creates a question in the viewer's mind that only the rest of the video can answer
- [ ] Hook is recorded as the very first thing in your recording session (when energy is highest)

## 5. Title Creation

> **Goal:** A title that makes someone stop scrolling and click.

### Title Rules
- [ ] Written primarily in Telugu (with stock/company name in English)
- [ ] Between 45–60 characters (longer gets cut off on mobile)
- [ ] Contains a number, percentage, or rupee amount when possible
- [ ] Contains an emotional trigger word (😳, shocking, secret, mistake, crash, opportunity)
- [ ] Does NOT start with the channel name
- [ ] Clearly states what the viewer will learn or feel

### Title Formula Templates

**Formula 1 — The Transformation**
> `₹[amount] → ₹[bigger amount] — [Company/Topic] తో ఇది ఎలా సాధ్యం?`

Example: `₹1 లక్ష → ₹15 లక్షలు — HDFC Bank తో ఇది ఎలా సాధ్యం?`

**Formula 2 — The Shocking Discovery**
> `[Company] గురించి ఈ [Shocking fact] ఎవరూ చెప్పలేదు 😳`

Example: `Reliance గురించి ఈ Number ఎవరూ చెప్పలేదు 😳`

**Formula 3 — The Decision Trigger**
> `[Company] ని ఇప్పుడు కొనాలా? నేను [My decision] చేశాను — ఎందుకంటే...`

Example: `Tata Motors ని ఇప్పుడు కొనాలా? నేను Buy చేశాను — ఎందుకంటే...`

**Formula 4 — The Common Mistake**
> `[X]% Investors చేసే ఈ Mistake మీరు చేయడం లేదు కదా? 😱`

Example: `90% Investors చేసే ఈ SIP Mistake మీరు చేయడం లేదు కదా? 😱`

**Formula 5 — The Crash Opportunity**
> `[Company] [X]% Crash అయింది — ఇది Buy Opportunity అా Trap అా?`

Example: `Infosys 35% Crash అయింది — ఇది Buy Opportunity అా Trap అా?`

**Formula 6 — The Specific Loss Warning**
> `ఈ Mistake వల్ల ₹[amount] నష్టపోయారు — మీరు కూడా చేస్తున్నారా?`

Example: `ఈ Mistake వల్ల ₹84 లక్షలు నష్టపోయారు — మీరు కూడా చేస్తున్నారా?`

### Title Checklist
- [ ] Written 3 title options — picked the most emotionally strong one
- [ ] Checked that the title fits in 60 characters
- [ ] Title contains at least one of: number, Telugu emotional word, or familiar company name
- [ ] Read the title aloud — does it sound like something you'd say to a friend?
- [ ] Searched the title on YouTube — is there too much identical competition?

## 6. Thumbnail Planning

> **Goal:** A thumbnail that makes someone click before they even read the title.

### Thumbnail Formula (Telugu Finance)
The proven layout:
- **Left side (50%):** Your face with a strong expression (surprise, excitement, pointing, concern)
- **Right side (50%):** 3–5 bold Telugu words OR a key number in massive font
- **Background:** Single bold color — deep red (#CC0000), dark orange, or navy blue
- **Bottom strip:** Company name or stock ticker in contrasting color
- **Corner:** TSI logo watermark (consistent on every video)

### Thumbnail Design Rules
- [ ] Your face is visible and takes up at least 40% of the thumbnail
- [ ] Maximum 5 words of text on the thumbnail (readers shouldn't need to read — they should feel)
- [ ] Text is at least 80px font size — readable on a phone screen
- [ ] High contrast between text and background
- [ ] Consistent brand colors used (pick 2–3 colors and use them always)
- [ ] TSI logo visible in corner
- [ ] No stock chart screenshots as the main element — they look the same as every other finance channel
- [ ] Thumbnail tells a different story from the title (together they create curiosity, alone each creates intrigue)
- [ ] Saved at 1280×720 pixels, JPG under 2MB

### Thumbnail Examples by Type

**Stock Analysis Thumbnail:**
- Your face looking surprised → Bold text: "₹500 → ₹5000?" → Company logo in corner

**Personal Finance Thumbnail:**
- Your face with a concerned expression → Bold text: "84 లక్షలు LOSS?" → SIP graphic

**Market Crash Thumbnail:**
- Your face looking at camera seriously → Red background → "Market కూలింది 😳"

**Mutual Fund Thumbnail:**
- Your face pointing at a number → Bold text: "ఇది కొనకండి!" → Fund name

### Thumbnail Checklist
- [ ] Designed before recording (this helps plan your facial expression on camera)
- [ ] Face expression matches the emotion of the video's hook
- [ ] Passed the "phone screen test" — looks readable when scaled down to thumbnail size
- [ ] Saved in /Thumbnails/ folder with the video's file name
- [ ] Shown to one other person — did they ask what the video is about? (Good = curiosity created)

## 8. Tags and Keywords

> **Goal:** Help YouTube understand exactly what your video is about.

### Tag Strategy
Use 8–15 tags per video. Mix these three types:

**Type 1 — Exact topic tags (3–4 tags)**
- The specific company or topic name
- Example: `HDFC Bank`, `HDFC Bank share analysis`, `HDFC Bank Telugu`

**Type 2 — Broad category tags (3–4 tags)**
- `Telugu stock market`, `Telugu investing`, `Indian stock market Telugu`, `Telugu mutual funds`

**Type 3 — Audience intent tags (3–4 tags)**
- `should I buy [stock]`, `[stock] buy or sell`, `best stocks 2026 Telugu`, `multibagger stocks Telugu`

**Type 4 — Channel brand tags (1–2 tags)**
- `Telugu Smart Investor`, `TSI`

### Tag Template by Video Type

**For stock analysis:**
```
[Company name], [Company] share analysis, [Company] Telugu, [Company] buy or sell,
Telugu stock market, stock analysis Telugu, Indian stock market, multibagger stocks,
Telugu investing, Telugu Smart Investor
```

**For mutual fund videos:**
```
best mutual funds Telugu, SIP investment Telugu, mutual fund analysis Telugu,
best SIP funds 2026, index funds Telugu, Telugu mutual funds, Telugu Smart Investor
```

**For personal finance:**
```
personal finance Telugu, money management Telugu, SIP vs [X], 
financial planning Telugu, wealth creation Telugu, Telugu Smart Investor
```

### Keyword Checklist
- [ ] Primary keyword used in title, first line of description, and first tag
- [ ] Tags include both Telugu and English versions of the topic
- [ ] No more than 15 tags (YouTube ignores channels that spam tags)
- [ ] Tags are relevant — no generic tags like "money" or "investment" alone

## 9. Chapters and Timestamps

> **Goal:** Let viewers navigate and signal to YouTube that your video has clear, organized structure.

### Why Chapters Matter
- YouTube shows chapter previews in search results (increases clicks)
- Viewers can skip to what they want (increases satisfaction)
- It forces you to structure your video before you film it

### Chapter Rules
- [ ] First chapter always starts at `0:00`
- [ ] Minimum 3 chapters, maximum 10 chapters for a 12-minute video
- [ ] Chapter titles are in Telugu or simple English — no jargon
- [ ] Each chapter is at least 60 seconds long
- [ ] Chapter titles create their own mini-curiosity arc

### Chapter Template — Stock Analysis Video

0:00 — పరిచయం (Introduction)
0:45 — [Company] అంటే ఏమిటి?
2:30 — Business ఎలా పని చేస్తుంది?

11:00 — నా అభిప్రాయం — Buy చేయాలా?

## 10. SEO Checklist

> **Goal:** Make your video findable on YouTube search for months after publishing.

### Pre-Upload SEO
- [ ] Primary keyword is in the **title** (ideally in the first 3 words)
- [ ] Primary keyword is in the **first 2 lines of description**
- [ ] Primary keyword is in the **first tag**
- [ ] Secondary keywords are in description body naturally
- [ ] Thumbnail file is named with the keyword (e.g., `hdfc-bank-analysis-telugu.jpg`)

### On-Page SEO
- [ ] Video title is 45–60 characters
- [ ] Description is at least 300 words
- [ ] 10–15 relevant hashtags added
- [ ] Custom thumbnail uploaded (default YouTube thumbnails reduce CTR by ~30%)
- [ ] Video added to the correct playlist
- [ ] Subtitles/captions added or uploaded (boosts SEO and accessibility)

### Captions Note
- [ ] Auto-generated captions reviewed and corrected — YouTube's auto-captions for Telugu are often wrong
- [ ] If possible, upload a `.srt` subtitle file for 100% accuracy

### Ranking Opportunity Checklist
Ask yourself before publishing:
- [ ] Is there a clear keyword people would type to find this video?
- [ ] Does your title match that keyword closely?
- [ ] Are there existing videos ranking for this keyword with fewer than 100K views? (Good opportunity)
- [ ] Did you include the current year (2026) in the title for time-sensitive topics?

## 11. File Naming Conventions

> **Goal:** Stay organized and never lose a file.

### Folder Structure
```
/YouTube/
  /[YYYY-MM-DD] [Topic Shortname]/
    /Raw Footage/
      raw_[topic]_take1.mp4
      raw_[topic]_take2.mp4
    /Assets/
      thumbnail_[topic].psd
      thumbnail_[topic]_final.jpg
      [any charts or screenshots used]
    /Edit/
      edit_[topic]_v1.prproj
      edit_[topic]_v2_final.prproj
    /Export/
      [YYYY-MM-DD]_[topic-keyword]_FINAL.mp4
    notes_[topic].txt
```

### File Naming Rules
- [ ] Use lowercase with hyphens (no spaces): `hdfc-bank-analysis.mp4`
- [ ] Always include the date: `2026-07-10_hdfc-bank-analysis-telugu_FINAL.mp4`
- [ ] Never name anything "final_final" or "v3_ACTUAL_final" — use version numbers: v1, v2, v3
- [ ] Thumbnail file named same as video file: `2026-07-10_hdfc-bank-analysis-telugu_THUMB.jpg`

## 12. Recording Checklist

> **Goal:** Clean, professional recording that saves editing time.

### Environment Setup
- [ ] Room is quiet — phone on silent, notifications off, fans and AC switched off or minimized
- [ ] Background is clean and uncluttered (or a consistent branded background)
- [ ] Lighting is in front of your face — not behind (backlight = dark face)
- [ ] Camera is at eye level — not below (looking up at the camera looks unprofessional)

### Audio Setup
- [ ] Microphone is close to your mouth (6–12 inches) and slightly off-axis (to reduce plosives)
- [ ] Do a 10-second test recording and listen back before starting
- [ ] Clap once at the start of each take (helps sync audio and video in editing)

### Camera Settings
- [ ] Resolution: 1920×1080 (1080p minimum)
- [ ] Frame rate: 30fps for talking head, 60fps if showing on-screen demos
- [ ] Auto-focus is on and working
- [ ] Battery is fully charged OR plugged in
- [ ] Enough storage space on card/device

### Performance Checklist
- [ ] Hook is recorded first — when your energy is highest
- [ ] Script is nearby but not being read word-for-word (natural delivery)
- [ ] Speaking pace is slightly slower than normal — viewers need time to absorb data
- [ ] Strong eye contact with the camera lens (not the screen)
- [ ] Recorded at least 2 takes of the hook
- [ ] Clapped or noted any mistakes during recording — do not stop and restart, keep rolling and redo the section

### Post-Recording
- [ ] All raw files backed up to a second location (external drive or cloud)
- [ ] Notes made on which takes are usable

## 13. Editing Checklist

> **Goal:** Engaging edit that retains viewers for 40%+ of the video.

### Pacing Rules
- [ ] Opening 30 seconds has NO dead air, no long intro, no music intro
- [ ] No single shot of your talking head longer than 45–60 seconds without a visual change
- [ ] Every section has at least one B-roll, screen recording, chart, or text overlay
- [ ] Pauses longer than 0.5 seconds are trimmed
- [ ] "Ums", "ahs", and repeated words are removed

### Visual Elements
- [ ] Charts and data are shown on screen when being discussed — not just described
- [ ] Text overlays highlight key numbers and facts
- [ ] Relevant stock charts from Screener.in or TradingView screen-recorded
- [ ] Transitions are simple (cut or quick fade — no flashy wipe transitions)
- [ ] End screen area (last 20 seconds) is free of text/graphics so cards can show

### Audio in Edit
- [ ] Background music is under 10% volume (subtle, not distracting)
- [ ] Music is copyright-free (YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay, or licensed tracks)
- [ ] Audio levels are consistent throughout — no sudden loud or quiet sections
- [ ] Normalize audio to -6dB to -3dB average loudness

### Retention Techniques
- [ ] "Pattern interrupt" every 45–90 seconds — B-roll, zoom, text animation, or graphic
- [ ] Micro-CTA at 2-minute mark: "Stay till the end — I'll tell you exactly what I would do"
- [ ] Final 60 seconds contain: summary → verdict → next video teaser → subscribe ask

### Editing Checklist Before Export
- [ ] First 30 seconds reviewed 3 times — this is the most important part
- [ ] Watch full video at 1.5x speed to catch dead air and pacing issues
- [ ] All text overlays spell-checked
- [ ] End screen added (last 20 seconds)
- [ ] Cards added at relevant moments
- [ ] Outro music fades out properly

## 14. Export Settings

> **Goal:** Best quality file at the smallest reasonable file size for fast uploading.

### Recommended Export Settings (Premiere Pro / DaVinci Resolve)

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Format | H.264 (.mp4) |
| Resolution | 1920 × 1080 |
| Frame Rate | Match source (25fps or 30fps) |
| Bitrate | VBR 2-pass, Target: 8 Mbps, Max: 12 Mbps |
| Audio Codec | AAC |
| Audio Sample Rate | 48,000 Hz |
| Audio Channels | Stereo |
| Audio Bitrate | 320 kbps |

### Export Checklist
- [ ] File exported to `/Export/` folder with correct file name
- [ ] File size is under 128GB (YouTube's maximum)
- [ ] Exported file played back to check for rendering errors
- [ ] Audio and video are in sync throughout

## 16. End Screens and Cards

> **Goal:** Keep viewers on your channel after the video ends.

### End Screen Setup (Last 20 seconds)
- [ ] "Best for viewer" video card — let YouTube choose the most relevant next video
- [ ] One specific video you want to promote (choose a video related to the current topic)
- [ ] Subscribe button element added
- [ ] End screen elements don't cover any text or graphics in the video

### Cards Setup
- [ ] Card at 20–30% mark of video — link to a related video ("If you liked this, watch this first")
- [ ] Card at 60–70% mark — link to your most popular video on this topic
- [ ] Card at 90% mark — link to a playlist

> **Tip:** Cards at 20–30% catch viewers who are engaged early. Cards at 60–70% re-engage viewers who are about to drop off.

## 17. Playlist Management

> **Goal:** Organized playlists that YouTube treats as a content "curriculum" — increasing session time.

### Playlist Structure for Telugu Smart Investor

| Playlist Name | What Goes In It |
|---|---|
| Multibagger Stock Picks | Deep dive stock analyses |
| Mutual Fund Guide Telugu | All SIP and mutual fund videos |
| Market Analysis Telugu | Weekly/monthly market recaps |
| Personal Finance Telugu | SIP vs loan, budgeting, emergency fund |
| Beginner's Investing Guide | 101-level explanatory videos |
| Sector Deep Dives | Defence, IT, EV, Pharma series |

### Playlist Rules
- [ ] Every video is added to at least one playlist immediately after upload

## 22. Analytics Checklist

> **Goal:** Know exactly what's working and what's not after each video.

### Check at 48 Hours
- [ ] **Impressions** — how many times did YouTube show your thumbnail?
- [ ] **CTR** — what % of impressions became clicks? (Target: 4%+)
- [ ] **Average View Duration** — how many minutes/seconds did people watch? (Target: 35–40% of video length)
- [ ] **Top Traffic Sources** — where are views coming from? (Search = good, Browse = good, Subscriber = expected)

### Check at 7 Days
- [ ] **Views vs Previous Video** — is this video performing better or worse?
- [ ] **Subscribers Gained** — did this video convert viewers to subscribers?
- [ ] **Audience Retention Graph** — where exactly do viewers drop off? (Fix that section next time)
- [ ] **Likes/Comments Ratio** — is engagement healthy? (1% of views = minimum)

### Check at 30 Days
- [ ] **Search ranking** — search your primary keyword. Where does your video appear?
- [ ] **Traffic source shift** — is Search traffic growing as a % of total? (It should be)
- [ ] **Top performing videos this month** — which 3 videos drove the most subscribers?

### Key Metrics Table

| Metric | Danger | Fair | Good |
|---|---|---|---|
| CTR | Below 2% | 2–4% | 4%+ |
| Avg view retention | Below 25% | 25–40% | 40%+ |
| Subscribers per 1000 views | Below 3 | 3–7 | 7+ |
| Likes per 100 views | Below 1 | 1–3 | 3+ |
| Comments per 100 views | Below 0.3 | 0.3–1 | 1+ |

### Analytics Action Rules
- **CTR below 2%?** → A/B test a new thumbnail and title immediately
- **Retention below 25%?** → Rewatch the first 60 seconds. Remake the hook next video.
- **Search traffic below 10%?** → Revisit keywords and title formula for next 3 videos
- **Same video getting more views after 2 weeks?** → It's ranking in search. Make more on this topic.

## 23. Common Mistakes to Avoid

> **Learn from others' failures before they become yours.**

### Content Mistakes
- [ ] ❌ Starting videos with "Hello everyone, welcome to my channel" — viewers skip this
- [ ] ❌ Spending the first 2 minutes explaining what the video is about — get to the point
- [ ] ❌ Covering too many topics in one video — focus on ONE insight per video
- [ ] ❌ Making videos about stocks nobody has heard of as your primary content
- [ ] ❌ Uploading every day when your subscriber base can't support that frequency
- [ ] ❌ Uploading Shorts that get less than 40% completion — they hurt your channel

### Packaging Mistakes
- [ ] ❌ Using English titles on a Telugu-audience channel
- [ ] ❌ Thumbnails with more than 5 words of text
- [ ] ❌ Not showing your face on thumbnails
- [ ] ❌ Changing your thumbnail style every video — inconsistency hurts brand recognition
- [ ] ❌ Titles longer than 60 characters (get cut off on mobile)

### SEO Mistakes
- [ ] ❌ Not adding timestamps/chapters
- [ ] ❌ Leaving the description nearly empty
- [ ] ❌ Using the same tags on every single video
- [ ] ❌ Not including the year in time-sensitive content titles
- [ ] ❌ Using auto-generated thumbnails

### Retention Mistakes
- [ ] ❌ Long uncut talking-head segments over 60 seconds with no visual change
- [ ] ❌ Reading from a script robotically — viewers can tell and disengage
- [ ] ❌ No B-roll, charts, or screen recordings to support what you're saying
- [ ] ❌ Burying the most interesting insight in the middle of the video — it should be in the hook
- [ ] ❌ Ending the video abruptly without a clear CTA

### Channel Strategy Mistakes
- [ ] ❌ Making 3 or 4 different types of content hoping one goes viral
- [ ] ❌ Chasing trending topics that don't match your niche
- [ ] ❌ Not responding to comments in the first 2 hours (hurts algorithm)
- [ ] ❌ Ignoring community posts — they keep your audience warm between uploads
- [ ] ❌ Comparing your month-3 channel to a year-7 channel
