## 🔴 AUTO-DISQUALIFIERS (Check First — If Any ONE is True, STOP)

- [ ] Auditor resignation or qualified / adverse audit opinion in last 2 years
- [ ] Proven promoter fraud (SEBI enforcement order, court conviction)
- [ ] Debt default or NPA classification
- [ ] CFO resignation within 12 months (without clear succession)
- [ ] Promoter pledge > 50% of total holding
- [ ] Negative OCF for 3 consecutive years (non-BFSI companies)
- [ ] Receivables > 180 days consistently
- [ ] SEBI investigation or ED/CBI raid on promoter/company in last 3 years
- [ ] Share count increased > 15% in 2 years without revenue growth justification
