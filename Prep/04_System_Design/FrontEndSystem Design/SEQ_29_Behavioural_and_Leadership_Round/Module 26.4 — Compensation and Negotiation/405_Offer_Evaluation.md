# 405 – Offer Evaluation Framework — Beyond the Number

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

## 1. ⚡ HIGH-LEVEL EXPLANATION
Total compensation is just one axis. Evaluate offers across 6 dimensions: **Compensation**, **Growth**, **Team/Manager**, **Tech Stack**, **Work-Life**, **Brand/Career**. Score each 1-10, weight by YOUR priorities. The highest-paying offer isn't always the best offer.

## 2. 🔬 DEEP-DIVE EXPLANATION

### 6-Dimension Evaluation Matrix
```
┌──────────────────────────────┬────────┬────────┬──────────┐
│ Dimension                    │ Co. A  │ Co. B  │ Weight   │
├──────────────────────────────┼────────┼────────┼──────────┤
│ 1. Total Compensation        │  /10   │  /10   │ 25%      │
│ 2. Growth & Learning         │  /10   │  /10   │ 20%      │
│ 3. Team & Manager Quality    │  /10   │  /10   │ 20%      │
│ 4. Tech Stack & Engineering  │  /10   │  /10   │ 15%      │
│ 5. Work-Life & Flexibility   │  /10   │  /10   │ 10%      │
│ 6. Brand & Career Impact     │  /10   │  /10   │ 10%      │
├──────────────────────────────┼────────┼────────┼──────────┤
│ WEIGHTED SCORE               │  /10   │  /10   │ 100%     │
└──────────────────────────────┴────────┴────────┴──────────┘
```

### What to Evaluate in Each Dimension

**1. Compensation (25%)**
- Total comp: base + bonus + RSU (calculate 4-year average)
- RSU vesting schedule (Amazon: 5/15/40/40 vs. Google: 25/25/25/25)
- Signing bonus and retention bonuses
- Health insurance quality, PF, perks value

**2. Growth (20%)**
- Promotion timeline and criteria clarity
- Internal mobility (can you switch teams/products?)
- Learning budget, conference attendance
- Mentorship and senior engineer access
- Scope of impact (greenfield vs. maintenance)

**3. Team & Manager (20%)**
- Manager's leadership style and reputation
- Team size and composition
- Team's reputation within the company
- Attrition rate (ask!) — high turnover = red flag
- Culture: collaborative or political?

**4. Tech Stack (15%)**
- Modern vs. legacy tech
- Engineering practices: CI/CD, testing, code review
- Infrastructure and tooling quality
- Technical debt reality (ask about tech debt in interviews)

**5. Work-Life (10%)**
- Remote/hybrid policy
- On-call expectations
- Meeting culture and focus time
- Vacation policy (planned vs. unlimited-but-not-taken)

**6. Brand (10%)**
- Resume value of the company name
- Industry respect and market position
- Company financial health and stability
- Does this role set up your NEXT move?

### Red Flags
```
🚩 High attrition in the team (>25% annually)
🚩 "We work hard, play hard" (code for overwork)
🚩 Manager hesitant about growth paths
🚩 Legacy stack with no modernization plans
🚩 Interviewer negativity about team/company
🚩 Offer pressure: "Accept by tomorrow"
🚩 Verbal promises not in written offer
```

### Decision Framework
```
1. Score each offer on all 6 dimensions
2. Apply YOUR weights (adjust based on career stage)
   - Early career: weight Growth and Brand higher
   - Mid career: weight Compensation and Tech higher
   - Senior: weight Team/Manager and Impact higher
3. Calculate weighted score
4. Sleep on it — does your gut agree with the math?
5. Negotiate the lower-scoring areas of your preferred offer
```

## 3. 🎯 KEY TAKEAWAY
**"Score offers across 6 dimensions weighted by your priorities. The highest TC isn't always the best offer. Growth, team quality, and career trajectory matter enormously. Get everything in writing. Sleep on it."**
