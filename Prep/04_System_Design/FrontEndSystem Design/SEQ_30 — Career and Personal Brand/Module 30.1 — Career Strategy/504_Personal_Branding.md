# 504 – Personal Branding for Senior Engineers

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

## 1. ⚡ HIGH-LEVEL EXPLANATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

**What it is:**
Personal branding is the strategic process of establishing yourself as a recognized expert in a specific domain. For senior frontend engineers targeting Big Tech, this means building visible credibility in areas like **web performance**, **accessibility**, **system design**, or **micro-frontends** through content, open source, and community engagement.

**Why it matters:**
At senior+ levels, inbound opportunities (recruiters reaching out) far outperform outbound applications. Engineers with visible expertise get referrals, skip resume screens, and negotiate better. Hiring managers Google your name — what they find determines if you're a "strong hire" signal before the interview.

## 5. ✅ WHY & HOW SUMMARY

**Why:** At senior+ levels, visible expertise creates inbound opportunities — referrals, headhunters, and skip-HR interview invites. Your brand is your leverage in negotiation.
**How:** Pick 1-2 niches → Write experience-based articles (not tutorials) → Optimize GitHub/LinkedIn → Engage in communities → Submit conference talks → Build relationships through DMs/coffee chats.
