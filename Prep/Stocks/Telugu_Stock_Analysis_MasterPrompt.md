**🔍 FRAMEWORK INTEGRATION — Advanced Valuation (Scoring Section 7):**

**Do NOT rely only on trailing P/E** — especially for early-stage or high-growth companies with suppressed/negative earnings. Apply these professional methods:

**Method 1: Revenue Multiple with Margin Normalization**
```
Implied Fair Value = Forward Revenue × Target Net Margin (at scale) × Justified P/E

Example:
  Revenue (next year) = ₹1,000 Cr
  Target Net Margin (Year 5) = 15%
  Justified P/E (growth adjusted) = 25x
  Implied Market Cap = ₹1,000 × 15% × 25 = ₹3,750 Cr
  
  Current market cap = ₹1,500 Cr → 2.5x upside to fair value
```

**Method 2: Price-to-Sales with Growth Adjustment**
```
Fair P/S = Peer Median P/S × (Company Growth Rate / Peer Median Growth Rate)

P/S < 3x for > 30% grower in large TAM = Acceptable
P/S > 10x needs > 50% growth AND path to > 20% margins = Caution
```

**Method 3: Scenario Analysis (Bull / Base / Bear)** — Present this visually:

| Scenario | Revenue (Y5) | Margin (Y5) | Multiple | Implied Value | Probability |
|----------|-------------|-------------|----------|---------------|-------------|
| 🟢 Bull | ₹X Cr | X% | Xx | ₹X Cr | 25% |
| 🟡 Base | ₹X Cr | X% | Xx | ₹X Cr | 50% |
| 🔴 Bear | ₹X Cr | X% | Xx | ₹X Cr | 25% |
| **Expected Value** | | | | **₹X Cr** | |

```
Expected Value = (0.25 × Bull) + (0.50 × Base) + (0.25 × Bear)
```

*"చూడండి... ఒక stock expensive అని అనడానికి PE ఒక్కటే చాలదు — ఆ company 5 years తర్వాత ఎంత earn చేయగలదో imagine చేయాలి..."*

**Method 4: Reverse DCF Sanity Check**
```
Current price implying ఎంత growth rate?
Implied growth < conservative estimate → Undervalued
Implied growth > 40% for 10+ years → Likely overvalued
```

**Valuation Red Lines:**

| Condition | Signal |
|-----------|--------|
| P/S > 20x with no path to profitability | ⚠️ Avoid |
| Market cap > TAM × reasonable market share | ⚠️ Avoid |
| Bull case already priced in at current price | ⏸️ Wait |
| Bear case offers < 30% downside | ✅ Attractive risk/reward |

**Important:** No "it's cheap/expensive therefore buy/sell" — only explain what the numbers mean and let viewers decide.
