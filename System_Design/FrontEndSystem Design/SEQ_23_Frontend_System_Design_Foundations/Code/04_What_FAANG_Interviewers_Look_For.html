💡 Hruday's Preparation Tips

Open every answer with scope: "Before designing X, I need to clarify: is this a consumer product or enterprise? What's the expected DAU? Any specific constraints?"
Name your trade-offs explicitly: "CSR is simpler to implement but gives worse FCP. SSR improves perceived performance but adds server costs. Given our requirement for SEO, I'd choose SSR."
Use your SAP experience as proof: Your Lighthouse 60→95 story IS the answer to "tell me about a performance optimization you drove." Don't hide your experience.
Think out loud deliberately: Interviewers can't evaluate what you don't say. Narrate your reasoning, not just your conclusion.
