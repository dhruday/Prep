# 395 – Story 6: Mentoring and Knowledge Sharing

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

## STAR Story

**SITUATION:** At SAP, two junior developers joined our team during the Fiori dashboard project. They had strong React backgrounds but no Angular or enterprise frontend experience. The project timeline didn't allow for extended onboarding.

**TASK:** I was tasked with ramping them up to productive contributors within 3 weeks while maintaining project velocity.

**ACTION:**
1. **Created a structured onboarding path** — 5-day Angular essentials bootcamp covering: TypeScript, Components/DI, RxJS basics, OnPush CD, and our codebase architecture
2. **Pair programming** — first 2 weeks, spent 1-2 hours/day pair programming on real tickets (not toy exercises). Started with simple bug fixes, graduated to feature stories
3. **Code review as teaching** — detailed PR reviews with explanations, not just "change this." Linked to documentation and Angular docs for each suggestion
4. **Created team patterns library** — documented our team's conventions: folder structure, naming, state management patterns, testing templates
5. **Assigned a "stretch" task** — each junior owned one complete feature end-to-end by week 3, with me as a safety net
6. **Weekly 1:1s** — asked about blockers, confidence level, and areas they wanted to grow in

**RESULT:**
- Both developers shipping features independently by week 3
- One of them implemented the accessibility component library (story 4) 6 months later
- Team velocity increased 40% with 3 productive developers vs. just me
- Patterns library used by 20+ developers across the org
- Both developers cited me as key mentor in their performance reviews
