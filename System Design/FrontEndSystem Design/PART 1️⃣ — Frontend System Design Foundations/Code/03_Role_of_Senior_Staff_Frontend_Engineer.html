👑 Role of a Senior / Staff Frontend Engineer

What separates Senior from Staff — Interview expectations at each level

L4 – Mid-Level Engineer
Execution Feature scope
Delivers features end-to-end within a well-defined scope. Writes tests, handles code reviews, raises blockers. Decisions are validated with senior engineer. Impact is at the individual feature level.
L5 – Senior Engineer (7+ years) ← YOU ARE HERE
Architecture Team impact Ownership
Owns entire features or systems end-to-end. Makes architectural decisions with clear trade-off documentation. Unblocks others. Sets technical standards. Impact is at the team level — 3-5 engineers aligned to your design decisions. Raises problems before they become incidents. This is the minimum bar to pass FAANG senior interviews.
L6 – Staff Engineer
Cross-team Vision Org impact
Defines technical vision and direction for multiple teams. Architects systems that span team boundaries (design systems, micro-frontend platforms, shared libraries). Bridges business requirements and technical possibility. Impact is at the org/product level — decisions affect 10+ engineers and 12–18 month roadmaps.
L7/Distinguished – Principal Engineer
Company-wide Industry influence
Defines engineering strategy across the entire company. Identifies 3–5 year technical investments. Represents the company in external technical communities. Makes 2–3 major technical decisions per year that affect the entire engineering organisation.

🎯 What FAANG Interviews Test at Senior Level

🔷 Ambiguity handling: Can you clarify requirements and make decisions without being told exactly what to build?
🔷 Trade-off articulation: Do you understand WHY you chose a solution, not just WHAT you chose?
🔷 Scale thinking: Does your design still work at 10× or 100× scale?
🔷 Cross-cutting concerns: Do you consider security, accessibility, and observability by default?
🔷 Production mindset: Error handling, monitoring, graceful degradation, rollback strategy.
🔷 Collaboration signal: Do you ask good questions? Do you build on interviewer suggestions?
The 60-second test: A senior engineer should be able to open any system design question with: "Before I dive in, let me clarify the requirements, then I'll walk through the high-level architecture, drill into the hardest parts, and address trade-offs." If you do this naturally, you're signalling senior-level thinking.
